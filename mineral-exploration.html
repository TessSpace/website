Why Tess Space

Turning field workflows into repeatable autonomous missions

Mineral exploration and remote site operations often depend on manual survey work, fragmented sensing workflows, and repeated field deployment across large and difficult terrain. Tess Space provides the autonomy layer that connects robotic mobility, onboard intelligence, mission planning, and sensor payloads into one operational workflow.

Harsh-environment validation

Greenland as a proving ground for remote exploration robotics

Greenland combines long distances, sparse infrastructure, difficult terrain, and demanding conditions with strong relevance for mineral exploration and geological field operations. For Tess Space, it is also a natural bridge between terrestrial remote-site robotics and the harsh-environment autonomy required in lunar analogue terrain.

Operating in these kinds of environments strengthens the value of robust perception, navigation, mission supervision, and repeatable field workflows. It is where autonomy must deliver practical performance, not only technical promise.
